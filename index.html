Argus Lab

Agents that see, act & verify.

“Lost, but moving forward.”

In Greek myth, Argus Panoptes was the hundred-eyed watchman — some eyes always open, nothing escaping his gaze. We build agents the same way: perceiving real interfaces, acting across them, and verifying every step of the work.

Research

the agentic stack, watched end to end
EYEP
Perception

Reading what interfaces actually render — charts, badges, maps, lazy-loaded and animating elements — on live webpages and desktop screens.

HANDA
Action

Executing non-trivial operations: sliders and date-range pickers, map drawing, video control, real mouse-and-keyboard automation across applications.

PROOFV
Verification

Verifiable agent-as-a-judge evaluation — rubric trees of independently checkable criteria, grounded in live sources, so scores mean what they claim.

02

Ethos

how we work

Watch the real thing. Agents should be studied on live interfaces, not sanitized sandboxes.

Claims need receipts. If an agent says it did the work, the work must be independently checkable.

Small group, long gaze. We would rather stare at one hard problem for a year than skim ten.

Lost, but moving forward. Not knowing the way is no reason to stand still.
