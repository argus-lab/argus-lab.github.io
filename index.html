Research

Three eyes on the agentic stack

From the browser to the desktop, we study how multimodal agents understand real interfaces, execute complex operations, and prove that they actually did the work.

👁

Perception

Reading what interfaces actually render — charts, badges, maps, lazy-loaded and animating elements — on live webpages and desktop screens.

⚡

Action

Executing non-trivial operations: sliders and date-range pickers, map drawing, video control, and real mouse-and-keyboard automation across applications.

✓

Verification

Verifiable agent-as-a-judge evaluation — rubric trees of independently checkable criteria, grounded in live sources, so scores mean what they claim.
